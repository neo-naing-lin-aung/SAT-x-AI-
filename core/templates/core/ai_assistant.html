Google Gemini

AI-Powered Learning Assistant

SAT × AI integrates Google's Gemini AI to provide additional learning support during question review. Students can ask questions, request simpler explanations, and explore alternative ways to understand a problem.

The goal is not simply to provide an answer, but to help students understand the reasoning behind it and develop their problem-solving skills.

How It Helps

When a question is difficult to understand, students can use the AI assistant to request step-by-step explanations and additional guidance.
