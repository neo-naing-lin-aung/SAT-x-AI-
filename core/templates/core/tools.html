SAT Study Tools

The Right Tools for Smarter SAT Prep

Explore trusted resources to strengthen your skills, practice SAT questions, and prepare for test day.

Learn. Practice. Prepare.

Preparing for the SAT takes more than answering questions. You need to understand important concepts, identify your weaknesses, and practice applying what you learn. These resources can help you build a more effective study routine.

RECOMMENDED RESOURCES

Start With These Two Tools

Free resources to support your SAT preparation.

Khan Academy

Learn and Strengthen Your Skills

Khan Academy offers free SAT preparation lessons and practice to help you understand concepts and improve your performance in Reading and Writing and Math.

What you can do
  • Review concepts you find difficult.
  • Practice specific SAT skills and question types.
  • Build a stronger foundation in Math, grammar, and reading.
  • Work on weaknesses before taking full-length tests.

Best for: Learning concepts and improving individual skills.

Explore Khan Academy

College Board

Official SAT Question Bank

The College Board is the organization that administers the SAT. Its official SAT Suite Question Bank lets you explore real SAT practice questions and filter them by subject, domain, skill, and difficulty.

What you can do
  • Practice official SAT questions.
  • Focus on specific domains and skills.
  • Choose questions based on difficulty.
  • Become more familiar with SAT question styles and expectations.

Best for: Practicing official questions and targeting specific weaknesses.

Explore Question Bank
STUDY STRATEGY

How to Use These Tools Together

Follow a simple cycle to make your practice more effective.

STEP 01
Learn the Concept

Use Khan Academy to study the rules, strategies, and concepts behind a question type.

STEP 02
Practice Official Questions

Use the College Board Question Bank to test your understanding with official SAT questions.

STEP 03
Review and Improve

Identify mistakes, revisit weak concepts, and practice again until you understand how to solve the questions.

PRACTICE WITH SAT × AI

Put What You Learn Into Practice

Use these resources to learn concepts and find practice questions. Then use SAT × AI to organize your practice sessions, track your answers, review mistakes, and get additional explanations.
