About SAT × AI

A Smarter Way to Practice for the SAT

SAT × AI is an educational project that combines SAT practice with explanations and AI-powered learning support.

WHAT IS SAT × AI?

Practice. Understand. Improve.

SAT × AI is a practice platform designed to help students practice SAT questions, understand their mistakes, and get additional help from AI when they need it.

Students can customize their practice sessions by choosing the topic, domain, difficulty, number of questions, and time limit. After completing a session, they can review explanations or ask the AI assistant for additional guidance.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built to Learn, Practice, and Experiment

SAT × AI is an educational project I built to learn and practice web development with Django. It combines my interests in software development, education, and artificial intelligence into one application.

While building this project, I am learning how to work with Django, databases, user authentication, templates, forms, and APIs. I am also exploring how AI can be integrated into an educational application to provide additional explanations and learning support.

This is primarily a learning and portfolio project. It is continuously being improved as I learn new technologies and develop better ways to help students practice and understand SAT questions.

THE GOAL

Turn Mistakes Into Progress

Getting a question wrong is part of learning. SAT × AI is designed to help students understand those mistakes, learn the underlying concepts, and become more confident with every practice session.
